Department profiles

For values that legitimately differ by which department initiated the RFQ - e.g. Department A's initiating officer name/designation, or its usual receiving store/authority, vs. Department B's. Each department can have one profile. When a document is generated for an RFQ, the app looks up that RFQ's department and overrides the organization-wide fields above with anything this profile sets (a profile only needs to define what's different - a blank field here still falls back to the organization-wide value). An RFQ with no department, or a department with no profile, just uses the organization-wide fields as-is.
